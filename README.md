# Olist E-Commerce Analysis

Analysis of 99,441 orders from Brazil's largest e-commerce marketplace 
(Sep 2016 – Oct 2018).

## Key Findings

- **3% repeat purchase rate** — 97% of customers bought once and never returned
- **Top 10% of products drive 61% of revenue** — severe SKU concentration risk  
- **93% on-time delivery** — but late orders score 2.71 vs 4.31 for early delivery
- **Top 10% of customers generate 41% of revenue**

## Three Business Recommendations

1. Launch post-purchase retention sequence targeting second order within 30 days
2. Redirect marketing to top SKU decile, audit bottom 50% of catalogue
3. Pad delivery estimates for high-risk sellers — conservative promises beat late delivery
